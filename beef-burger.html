Beef Burgers

Juicy homemade beef burgers with all the fixings Carnivore

⏱ Prep: 15 min
Cook: 12 min
🍽 4 servings
👨‍🍳 Easy

Ingredients

  • 600g ground beef
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Burger buns
  • Optional: cheese, lettuce, tomato

Instructions

1
Combine beef, onion, garlic, egg, and breadcrumbs.
2
Add Worcestershire sauce, salt, and pepper.
3
Mix gently and form into patties.
4
Preheat grill or skillet to medium-high.
5
Cook patties 5–6 minutes per side.
6
Add cheese during last minute if desired.
7
Toast buns lightly.
8
Assemble burgers and serve.
