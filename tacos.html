Tacos

Quick and easy tacos with seasoned meat and fresh toppings Quick & Easy

⏱ Prep: 10 min
Cook: 10 min
🍽 4 servings
👨‍🍳 Easy

Ingredients

  • 500g ground beef or chicken
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 8–10 taco shells or tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • Salsa to taste
  • Fresh cilantro and lime wedges

Instructions

1
Brown ground meat in a skillet over medium-high heat.
2
Drain fat and add taco seasoning with 1/4 cup water.
3
Simmer 5 minutes until sauce thickens.
4
Warm taco shells according to package directions.
5
Fill shells with seasoned meat.
6
Top with lettuce, tomatoes, cheese, and sour cream.
7
Add salsa and garnish with cilantro.
8
Serve with lime wedges.
