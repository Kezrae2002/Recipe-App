Brownies

Rich and fudgy chocolate brownies Dessert

⏱ Prep: 10 min
Bake: 25 min
🍽 8 servings
👨‍🍳 Easy

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/3 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp baking powder
  • Optional: chocolate chips or walnuts

Instructions

1
Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2
Melt butter and mix with sugar.
3
Add eggs and vanilla; whisk until smooth.
4
Stir in cocoa powder, flour, salt, and baking powder.
5
Fold in chocolate chips or nuts (optional).
6
Spread batter into a greased baking pan.
7
Bake for 25 minutes and cool before cutting.
