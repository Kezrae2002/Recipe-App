Vegan Chili

Hearty and spicy plant-based chili with beans and vegetables Vegan

⏱ Prep: 15 min
Cook: 40 min
🍽 6 servings
👨‍🍳 Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 bell peppers, diced
  • 2 cans kidney beans, drained
  • 2 cans black beans, drained
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons chili powder
  • 1 tablespoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh cilantro for garnish

Instructions

1
Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2
Sauté onion and garlic until softened (about 5 minutes).
3
Add bell peppers and cook for another 5 minutes.
4
Stir in chili powder, cumin, and paprika; cook 1 minute.
5
Add beans and crushed tomatoes; bring to a boil.
6
Reduce heat and simmer 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
7
Season with salt and pepper to taste.
8
Garnish with cilantro and serve hot.
